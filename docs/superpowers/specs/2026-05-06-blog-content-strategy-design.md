# Blog Content Strategy

## Positioning

> **"A senior platform engineer's perspective on building and working in the AI era"**

- **Target audience:** Senior+ engineers, Engineering Managers, Architects, Tech Leads
- **Differentiator:** Not a tutorial writer, not a pure architect — the perspective of someone who can ship code *and* see the platform whole
- **Primary goals:** Personal brand (thought leadership) + career positioning
- **Scope:** English tech blog only. Theme "普通人在AI时代" goes to 掘金 + WeChat (老奥杂说) exclusively.
